About the Legal Entrance Examinations (CLAT, AILET & LSAT)

Admission into premier legal streams relies heavily on major national and state-level entrance assessments. The Common Law Admission Test (CLAT) serves as the benchmark gateway for undergraduate (5-year Integrated LLB) and postgraduate (LLM) admissions across National Law Universities (NLUs) in India. Along with CLAT, exams like AILET (for NLU Delhi) and LSAT India open doors to premier private legal schools.

Cracking these exams requires more than rote memorization; it requires sharp critical reasoning, legal aptitude, and time management. Furthermore, choosing the right counseling options based on your rank prevents seat forfeiture. Eligibility Criteria for Law Admissions

Before finalizing your applications for law schools, review the baseline eligibility requirements regulated by the Bar Council of India (BCI) and university admission boards:

For 5-Year Integrated LLB

Must have completed Class 12th (or equivalent) from any recognized stream (Science, Commerce, Arts/Humanities) with a minimum aggregate of 45% to 50% marks.

For 3-Year LLB (Graduate Entry)

Candidate must hold a graduation degree (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com, B.Tech, etc.) in any discipline from a recognized university with required minimum qualifying percentages.

Age Restrictions

As per BCI regulations, there is no upper age limit for appearing in CLAT UG/PG or enrolling in general undergraduate and postgraduate law courses in India.

Entrance Exam Qualification

Qualifying state or national law entrance exams (CLAT, AILET, MH CET Law, LSAT) with competitive marks is mandatory for premier college seats.
