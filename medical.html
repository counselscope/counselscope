About the Medical Entrance Examination (NEET-UG)

In India and for international equivalents recognized by medical boards, the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET-UG) serves as the singular gateway for admission into undergraduate medical and allied programs. Conducted annually, this standardized test evaluates students on Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Botany & Zoology).

Securing a competitive score in NEET requires not just hard work, but a smart counseling strategy. Even with a decent score, students often miss out on premier government or private seats due to poor choice-filling or lack of awareness regarding state vs. All India Quota (AIQ) counseling guidelines. Eligibility Criteria for Medical Admissions

Before locking your choices for medical counseling, it is crucial to verify that you fulfill all regulatory eligibility benchmarks mandated by medical education boards:

Academic Qualification

Must have passed Class 12th (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, and English as core subjects from a recognized board.

Minimum Aggregate Marks

General category candidates typically require a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in PCB, with relaxed criteria for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC).

Age Limit

Candidate must have completed 17 years of age as of December 31st of the year of admission. There is no upper age limit for NEET-UG.

Entrance Exam Score

Qualifying the NEET-UG examination with the requisite cutoff percentile is mandatory for all medical and dental seats in India.
